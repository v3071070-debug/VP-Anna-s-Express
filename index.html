Our Story

At VP Anna's Express, we serve authentic Sri Lankan and South Indian dishes that taste just like home. From fragrant rice and rich curries to crispy dosas and spicy sambals, every meal is cooked with care, tradition, and bold flavours.

Our food is made fresh daily, using family recipes and quality ingredients that bring comfort with every bite. Whether you're craving a hearty mutton curry or a light tiffin meal — we've got you.
